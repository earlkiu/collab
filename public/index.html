Collaboration

Some pictures need no brief

Every so often I want to photograph someone with nothing attached to it — no client, no deadline, no idea of what it should look like before we start. Just a room, a long afternoon, and enough quiet for the performance to fall away.

That's what this is. Unpaid on both sides. You bring yourself. I bring the camera and the patience.

If any of that sounds like something you've been waiting for, tell me a little below. I read every one of these.

01 — Who you are

These sessions are for adults only — 18 and over.
You'll need to be 18 or older to submit this form. If you'd still like to work together, write to me at hello@earlkiu.com with a parent or guardian included.

02 — What I'd like to know

This is the part I read first. There's no right answer, and I'd rather have three honest lines than a paragraph you think I want.
Up to three. Leave it empty if there isn't one — that's an answer too.
Images, films, a song, a person, a mood — whatever holds a version of you you'd like to see.

03 — Practical things

Where you're comfortable Required Select the furthest you'd be willing to go. Nothing here is a commitment — we'd talk it through properly before any shoot, and you can change your mind at any point, including on the day.
Wardrobe, locations, how the images are used, anything at all. Better said now than on the day.

04 — The arrangement

Neither of us is paid. You receive between 6 and 12 finished images, edited and delivered digitally, usually within three to four weeks.

Those images are yours to keep and to post — personally, socially, and in your own portfolio. They aren't to be used commercially, sold, or altered with crops, filters or edits of any kind. You're welcome to show them when applying for representation, but an agency can't use or distribute them without my permission. The frame is part of the picture.

On my side, I keep the right to use the images however I choose — portfolio, print, exhibition, publication, editorial, promotion, online. That's the trade, and it's the whole reason the session costs nothing.

Everything else — wardrobe, location, what we're making — we work out together before the day.

Please confirm all four before submitting.
I reply to everyone, though not always quickly.
